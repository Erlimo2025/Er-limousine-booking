PLANNING YOUR AIRPORT RIDE

Newark Airport car service FAQs

Can I book pickup from Newark Airport (EWR)?

Yes. This service starts at Newark Liberty International Airport in New Jersey. Select the airport and your terminal, then enter your destination and pickup schedule.

Is the $150 flat rate only for EWR to Manhattan?

Yes. It applies only to the existing qualifying one-way EWR → Manhattan Luxury SUV offer using the Chevrolet Suburban Premier. The booking system verifies EWR pickup and Manhattan destination eligibility. It does not apply to other destinations, vehicles or reverse trips, and FIRST15 is excluded.

Can I travel from EWR to destinations in New Jersey?

Yes. Enter your New Jersey destination for a normal quote based on your actual route, schedule and vehicle.

Can I book EWR transportation to Connecticut?

Yes. Select your EWR pickup and actual Connecticut destination, then review the normal quote calculated for your trip.

Can I book EWR transportation to Pennsylvania?

Yes. Add your EWR pickup and actual Pennsylvania destination in the booking form. Review your route and normal quote before reserving.

Can I reserve now and pay later?

Yes. Choose Reserve & Pay Later after reviewing your quote. Your reservation is saved as Unpaid and payment is still required. Signed-in customers can use Complete Payment in My Trips for an eligible unpaid trip.

Can I change my airport pickup time?

Signed-in customers can change the date or time of an eligible active reservation in My Trips. Choose a future pickup time; cancelled or completed reservations cannot be changed.

What vehicles are available?

Our current booking options include the Chevrolet Suburban Premier and Cadillac Escalade ESV, each for up to 6 passengers. The Manhattan special is limited to the Suburban; the Escalade uses a normal quote.
