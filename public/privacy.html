Customer Review Privacy Policy

This policy explains how ER Limousine Service handles customer reviews.

Your invitation and feedback

After an administrator marks your trip completed, we may email a private review invitation to the email address stored on that reservation. Providing a rating or written feedback is optional. A review does not create an account or change your booking or payment.

What may appear publicly

By submitting a review, you provide feedback that may be published on this website after administrator approval. Published reviews show your original 1–5 star rating, optional comment, and first name with last initial, or an anonymous display name. We do not publish your email, phone number, reservation reference, trip route or payment information.

Please leave private addresses, contact information and other sensitive details out of your comment. Administrators review feedback for authenticity, spam, privacy and inappropriate content using the same rules for all ratings. Reviews are not edited to change their rating or meaning.

Private handling and delivery

Authorized administrators can see the review, customer name, reservation reference and moderation history. Review invitations use our existing Resend email service. We store the invitation's cryptographic token hash, feedback and delivery/moderation records in our reservation database; the private invitation token is not stored there. The invitation expires 30 days after delivery is first attempted and permits one review for that completed trip.

Review and audit records are retained for moderation and service integrity and are not automatically erased when a review is kept private or removed from public display. Database and backup access remains restricted.

Questions or removal requests

Use the ER Limousine Service contact details to ask about your review or request its removal from public display. Please do not send payment credentials or your private invitation link.
