Customer Privacy Notice

Local draft — pending final review and not approved for publication

This notice describes how ER Limousine Service handles information when you use this website, book a ride, manage an account or reservation, or leave a review.

Information we collect and why

  • Contact information. We use your name, email address and phone number to make and manage reservations, coordinate rides and respond to requests. We use your email address to send booking confirmations, payment links and other service messages when applicable.
  • Trip information. We collect pickup and destination addresses, intermediate stops, trip dates and times, and any airport, terminal, flight or reservation notes you provide. We use the locations and timing to look up routes and calculate fares. We use trip details to coordinate and provide rides.
  • Account information. If you register, we use your contact information, password and email verification to create and protect your account. We store a password hash, which lets us check your password without keeping the original. We use your email address for password recovery.
  • Payment and service records. We keep booking references, amounts, payment status and confirmations, payment provider references, cancellations, refunds and requests about your ride. These support payment processing, reconciliation, customer service, dispute handling, accounting and applicable recordkeeping obligations.
  • Device and security information. We use information such as your IP address, session information and limited error or request details to operate the website, limit abusive activity, prevent fraud and investigate service problems.
  • Reviews. If you choose to leave a rating or written feedback, we use it for review moderation and possible publication as described below.

Accounts, guest bookings and trip history

New account registration uses email verification. You can sign in to use My Trips for reservations linked to your account and use email recovery if you forget your password and recovery is available.

You can book as a guest without an account. Guest access can use a booking-specific cookie or an emailed verification link when the booking reference and email match. Links and temporary access expire. A retained guest booking may still qualify for a new verification link.

We keep trip records, which can include exact locations and schedules. Authorized Dispatch staff use itinerary details to provide rides and handle booking questions or changes. Trip details can also appear in booking communications and in reservation views for customers with access. ER Limousine does not currently assign reservations to independent chauffeurs or outside limousine companies. Authorized staff and the service providers described below may still handle information for their work.

When you view a completed booking, the booking response omits the driver’s phone number. Cancelled booking responses omit driver assignment details. Authorized Dispatch staff retain operational access to assignment information. These display limits leave the underlying business records in place.

Payments and service providers

  • Stripe processes payments and handles card entry. Our application does not store full card numbers or card security codes (CVC) as part of payment processing. Stripe receives information needed to process or reconcile a payment, such as the amount, booking reference, and the applicable email address or Stripe customer reference. New ordinary Checkout sessions use a generic service description. Some payment links created by Dispatch and some older saved payment attempts may include pickup, destination and scheduled departure details. We keep payment references and confirmation records to reconcile payments and handle refunds and disputes.
  • Google Places, Geocoding and Routes process address searches, selected places and trip inputs such as pickup, destination, stops and departure time for address lookup and routing. If you choose to use your current location and grant browser permission, we may send your location coordinates to Google to look up an address.
  • Resend delivers service emails to customers and staff. It receives the recipient address and message contents, which may include reservation details, payment links or links for account recovery, verification or guest access.
  • Render hosts our website application and PostgreSQL database. Website requests and information in the application and database are processed as part of that service.
  • Yahoo hosts our privacy request mailbox. When you email us there, Yahoo handles your email address and the contents of your message. Authorized ER Limousine staff handle requests manually.

Service providers can hold their own records. A change to our records does not automatically remove copies held by a provider or in an email inbox. Provider retention and deletion periods are still to be verified.

Cookies, browser storage and tracking

We use cookies to support signing in, account verification and recovery, and access to a quote or booking. We also use session and request information to protect those features from abuse. Your browser can keep a reference for a saved-card setup attempt in session storage.

If you opt in to remembering guest booking contact details, your browser saves your name, email address and phone number for future guest bookings. This feature stores contact details only, with no automatic expiry set by our website. Use Forget my details on the booking form or clear this site's data in your browser to remove the saved information.

When a customer chooses to set up a saved payment card, the website loads Stripe.js to securely handle the setup. Authorized ER Limousine Dispatch staff can view internal trip and payment reports through Business Analytics. These reports are used only for internal business operations.

Our review of the website code found no advertising pixels or visitor analytics tags. We have yet to verify tracking added outside that code or usage data collected by providers.

Retention and security

We may retain information to provide transportation, manage accounts, reconcile payments, handle refunds and disputes, prevent fraud and meet applicable obligations. Some access links and security records expire or are cleared under specific rules.

  • Completed and cancelled reservations. We review retained records five years from the final scheduled trip date. We preserve payment, refund, dispute and accounting evidence when required.
  • Abandoned unpaid reservations. We review records 30 days after abandonment is confirmed, once the final scheduled trip leg has elapsed and applicable payment and dispute checks are complete.

These checkpoints call for review only. They do not trigger automatic cancellation, archiving, deletion or removal from My Trips. The business owner approves any disposal. Stored business records can remain after they are no longer visible in My Trips. The five-year checkpoint does not guarantee access for five years or indefinitely. The website has no automatic account-erasure process.

We retain accounting, payment, tax, insurance, transportation and contractual records according to applicable obligations. The business owner authorizes placement and release of legal holds. Staff document holds and preserve relevant records while a hold is active. When required and technically possible, this includes considering exports, backups, mailbox copies and provider records.

We use safeguards such as sign-in requirements, limited access to reservation views, password hashing and temporary access links to help protect customer information.

Privacy questions and requests

Email erlimousinellc@yahoo.com to report a privacy concern, ask a question or request access to, correction of or deletion of information where applicable. Tell us what you are asking for and how to reach you. Please do not send passwords, card details or private access links.

Authorized ER Limousine staff monitor the privacy mailbox, acknowledge requests promptly and track applicable legal response deadlines. Staff verify your identity and authority before disclosing or deleting information. The business owner reviews every request and approves the final decision and response. Staff communicate securely and document requests and outcomes in a secure log. This is a manual process.

What we can provide, change or delete depends on applicable law and why we need the record. Some information may need to be kept for a reservation, payment, refund, dispute or other obligation. For review removal, see the review section below.

Customer Review Privacy Policy

This policy explains how ER Limousine Service handles customer reviews.

Your invitation and feedback

After an administrator marks your trip completed, we may email a private review invitation to the email address stored on that reservation. Providing a rating or written feedback is optional. A review does not create an account or change your booking or payment.

What may appear publicly

By submitting a review, you provide feedback that may be published on this website after administrator approval. Published reviews show your original 1–5 star rating, optional comment, and first name with last initial, or an anonymous display name. Public review records exclude structured reservation fields such as email, phone number, reservation reference, trip route and payment details. A public comment may include information you choose to write in it.

Please leave private addresses, contact information and other sensitive details out of your comment. Administrators review feedback for authenticity, spam, privacy and inappropriate content using the same rules for all ratings. Reviews are not edited to change their rating or meaning.

Private handling and delivery

Authorized administrators can see the review, customer name, reservation reference and moderation history. Review invitations use our existing Resend email service. We store a cryptographic hash of the invitation token, feedback and delivery and moderation records in our reservation database. The private invitation token is not stored there. The invitation expires 30 days after delivery is first attempted and permits one review for that completed trip.

Review and audit records are retained for moderation and service integrity and are not automatically erased when a review is kept private or removed from public display. Our storage policy calls for restricting database and backup access.

Questions or removal requests

Email erlimousinellc@yahoo.com to ask about your review or request its removal from public display. Please do not send payment credentials or your private invitation link.
