How these numbers are calculated
Bookings received and gross accepted value
Based on when each reservation was created. The gross value includes unpaid and cancelled reservations.
Ride activity and trip report
Based on scheduled pickup date and current Dispatch status. Upcoming counts future confirmed, assigned and en-route reservations. Pending means awaiting payment. Cancellation rate is cancelled reservations divided by all scheduled reservations in the range.
Customer requests
Change and cancellation requests submitted in the range, grouped by their current decision status.
Completed fares and collectible balances
Completed fares use stored fares for rides marked Completed. Balances use the accepted fare less verified captures for noncancelled trips. Unresolved payment evidence may make a balance unavailable.
Payments, refunds and net collected
Based on application-recorded Stripe confirmation dates. Net collected is confirmed captures less confirmed refunds. External activity without verifiable local evidence and Stripe fees are outside these totals.
Prior period
Whole-month selections compare the same number of preceding full calendar months. Other selections compare the same number of preceding New York calendar days. Weeks start Monday.
