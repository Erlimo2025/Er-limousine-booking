PLANNING YOUR RIDE

Frequently asked questions

Can I book from New Jersey to Manhattan?

Yes. Enter your pickup in New Jersey and your actual Manhattan destination in the booking form. Review your route, date, time and quote before reserving.

Can I reserve now and pay later?

Yes. Choose Reserve & Pay Later after reviewing your quote. Your trip is reserved as Unpaid, and payment is still required.

Can I change my pickup time?

Signed-in customers can change the pickup date or time in My Trips while the reservation is active. The new pickup must be in the future; cancelled or completed trips cannot be changed.

What vehicles are available?

The current fleet includes the Chevrolet Suburban Premier and Cadillac Escalade ESV, each accommodating up to 6 passengers. Choose your vehicle and review its quote in the booking form.

How do I complete payment later?

Signed-in customers can open My Trips and select Complete Payment for an eligible unpaid reservation. Payment continues through secure Stripe Checkout. Your reservation shows Paid once payment is confirmed.
