# Private Suburban and NJ E-ZPass acceptance evidence

No private account, vehicle or settled statement was inspected in preparing this
package. All operator classification/history remains **unverified**. Provide
redacted copies privately for review; **do not upload them to Git/GitHub or put
them into public infrastructure JSON**. No login, password, OTP, full tag/account
number, full plate/VIN, banking or card details are needed. Use one private
vehicle alias and one tag alias consistently across the selected records.

The NJ E-ZPass public site identifies `Tags/Vehicles` (Tag List / Vehicle List),
`My Account` (Account Profile / Plans / Service Request) and `Activity`
(Transactions / Statements / Correspondence). Availability can differ by account.
If the portal does not show a required fact, request written agency confirmation
instead of guessing or changing a setting.
[NJ E-ZPass public help](https://www.ezpassnj.com/en/about/faqs.shtml)

## 1. Link the actual vehicle, tag and account

Provide a redacted Vehicle List entry for the **2025 Chevrolet Suburban**, its
registered body/plate class (NJ OL), and the associated active Tag List entry.
Keep only enough consistent masked suffix/alias information to demonstrate the
same vehicle/tag association. Show NJ issuer/business account type and relevant
plan names/statuses, with evidence date and any expiry. A business account and
`2Ax` description alone do not prove the charged PA, NJTA or MTA class.

Provide physical/registration evidence of two axles, single rear wheels, no
trailer, passenger configuration/seating and registered maximum gross weight.
A redacted registration/manufacturer label or suitable photo can establish those
facts without exposing VIN, home address or full plate. If accessories/body
changes or vehicle assignment differ, review again. Confirm the tag is mounted
as directed and is actively read; registration alone does not prove readable use.

## 2. Obtain agency-specific written class confirmation

Use NJ E-ZPass `Service Request`/Correspondence or the authority's customer
service channel, privately supplying their required identifiers. Request this
specific information and retain the redacted answer/reference and date:

> For my identified 2025 Chevrolet Suburban, NJ OL registration, NJ-issued
> E-ZPass Business tag and two-axle/single-rear-wheel configuration with no
> trailer, which class and plan are actually assigned for Port Authority,
> NJTA Turnpike/Parkway and MTA Bridges and Tunnels? Does it qualify for the
> published NY/NJ PA Class 1 readable-tag tariffs and NJTA passenger off-peak
> rate? Which MTA class and non-NYCSC rate applies to its registered MGW/body
> category? Please explain any different class or tag-read/plate-based billing.

This is a suggested operator inquiry, not a message sent by the application.
The authority's response plus matching settled transactions must support the
mapping. PA's two-axle/single-rear-wheel definition, NJTA's Class 1 definition
and MTA's Class 31/32 definitions are separate; never inherit one from another.
Do not apply NYCSC discounts to an NJ-issued account merely because E-ZPass works.
[PA table](https://www.portauthoritybuilds.com/bridges-tunnels/en/tolls.html),
[NJTA classes](https://www.njta.gov/nj-e-zpass-class-definitions/),
[NJTA discounts](https://www.njta.gov/toll-discounts/),
[MTA classes and issuer rules](https://www.mta.info/fares-tolls/tolls/vehicle-types)

## 3. Establish the congestion plan and credit terms

Provide applicable redacted Plans/enrollment/vehicle status or written MTA/NJ
E-ZPass confirmation that the identified vehicle is on the **standard daily
passenger/small-commercial CRZ treatment**, including any exemption, discount,
credit restriction or different class. Confirm its actual plate/tag association
for CRZ billing. Operator-reported non-TLC status is recorded; it has not been
independently verified. NJ OL plates or a commercial limousine business do not
establish NYC TLC Per-Trip Charge Plan enrollment. Do not enroll or change a plan
as part of this acceptance exercise.

Request current written clarification of:

* Peak Lincoln/Holland credit eligibility and readable-tag/plate conditions.
* Queens-Midtown/Carey eligibility for the published $1.50 Phase 1 peak per-trip
  entry/exit credit; Lincoln/Holland eligibility for the $3 peak entry credit.
  The published daily credit maximum is $5. Amounts are independently confirmed;
  actual entitlement and complete remaining allowance still need evidence.
* Same-vehicle repeat entry, overnight then peak, later eligible tunnel credits,
  combined daily credit limit, midnight/calendar-day reset and posting delays.
* Any currently applied date-specific changes, gridlock adjustments or exemption.

[MTA daily rules](https://www.mta.info/fares-tolls/tolls/congestion-relief-zone/about),
[detailed schedule](https://www.mta.info/document/138931),
[PTCP eligibility](https://www.mta.info/fares-tolls/tolls/congestion-relief-zone/taxi-fhv-tolls)

## 4. Supply settled comparison rows for chosen acceptance trips

For each selected route/direction, provide only the relevant redacted settled
transaction rows and associated later credits/adjustments. Keep:

* Actual use date **including year**, use time and timezone; separately retain
  posting date/time. Do not price from posting time.
* Agency, actual facility/plaza name **and code**, direction, charged class,
  entry and exit interchanges/times where present, and masked vehicle/tag alias.
* Gross toll debit, separate CRZ debit, separate crossing credit, other road
  tolls, reversals/adjustments, currency, final status and an opaque review ref.
* For CRZ daily comparisons, the relevant entries/credits for this same vehicle
  for that New York calendar day, including prior/overnight entries. Other
  vehicles' fleet charges do not establish this vehicle's history.
* Complete gross daily charged cents and already credited cents, including later
  settled adjustments, plus the evidence's actual covered-through time. A flag
  saying paid is insufficient. Unknown/future history and overnight-first peak
  credits stay manual; do not extend validity or assume first entry.
* The actual dispatched vehicle, intended route and any detour. A generic Luxury
  SUV product or a previous day's statement cannot prove future vehicle/history.

Begin with Lincoln and Holland peak/off-peak, GWB outbound/reverse, one applicable
NJTA ticket pair, and a day with repeat CRZ entries/credits. Extend to NYC
crossings only after their rate and geometry evidence is reviewed. Compare
Google's original aggregate separately from official components and net settled
charges. Neither difference nor a missing Google amount may be invented as zero.

## 5. Investigate the reported anomalies

For the **$19.55 Holland** observation, obtain the year, true usage versus posting
time, facility/code, charged class, tag-read versus plate-processing method and
any later adjustment. $19.55 matches the published 2026 PA Class 1 Mid-Tier
reference, but the amount/time alone is not proof of Mid-Tier or its cause.
Obtain the authority's explanation if the transaction detail omits the method.

For **Union Ramp $2.25**, obtain the exact agency/plaza code, direction, usage
date/year and class. NJTA's 2026 table has both Union mainline NB (142.7) and
Union Ramp NB entrance (142.8) at conditional Class 1 $2.25. Confirm which
collection point and any overlap/adjustment actually applied. Do not sum both
or infer a plaza from an amount. Investigate missing entry/exit reads, maximum
fare or other abnormal charges separately from ordinary route tariffs.

## Evidence outcome

The reviewer should record only an opaque evidence reference, applicability,
reviewer/date, expiry and approved vehicle alias in a private audit record. No
full identifiers, statement scans or route history belong in source control.
Vehicle/class/tag evidence does not by itself approve road geometry, daily facts,
credits, timing bounds or customer activation. All of those gates remain separate.
